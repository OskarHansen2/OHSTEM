Our Mission

Many talented students never receive guidance on research, college pathways, graduate school preparation, internships, or STEM careers.

STEM Pathways Network connects students with volunteer mentors who have already navigated these challenges and can provide personalized advice.

Who Can Apply?

  • Industry professionals

Expected Commitment

  • 1 meeting per month
  • Email support
  • Professional guidance
  • Career advice
